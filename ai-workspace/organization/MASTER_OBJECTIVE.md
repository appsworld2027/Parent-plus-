Source: founder-provided CODEX MASTER OBJECTIVE in the task attachment. This is the operational tracker, not a separate ChatGPT Goal.

## Outcome
Establish and validate an on-demand, cloud-based AI development organization using Codex, GitHub, Cezar, Superpowers and Spec Kit where compatible. Preserve Parent Plus code. Keep production systems untouched. No paid service, protected-branch merge or production change without explicit approval.

## Organization
- Founders Ankit and Manu retain product and release decisions and equal visibility.
- Bot Asha — AI Product Manager / CPTO: prioritization, requirements, architecture coordination, conflict analysis and reporting.
- Aurora Parent: Bot Neha (project manager), Bot Tara (database), Bot Riya (integrations), Bot Ishan (implementation), Bot Meera (independent QA).
- Coach & Workspace: Bot Dev (project manager), Bot Farah (database), Bot Kabir (integrations), Bot Arjun (implementation), Bot Vikram (independent QA).
- Each role needs documented responsibilities, inputs, outputs, tools, permissions, escalation rules and measurable success criteria. Roles run on demand.

## Operating rules
- GitHub is the shared source of truth for requirements, decisions, tasks, status and evidence.
- Isolate concurrent work by branch/worktree. Assign module ownership and flag overlapping paths/contracts before dispatch.
- AI reviews and tests work. Required checks and human approvals gate merges.
- Daily status includes completed/active/pending/blocked work, validation, decisions, risks and usage/cost.
- Track actual usage when provider exposes it. Mark estimates clearly. No new paid service.
- Keep production, hosted data, real child records and secrets out of the pilot.
- Do not merge or change protected branches, production services or paid plans without explicit approval.
